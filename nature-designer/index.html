NatureDesigner

Post-human Society Virtual Clothing Design

In the post-human era, nature reclaims the power to shape. This project explores how ecological intelligence, represented by mycelium and slime molds, can generate form and structure autonomously. By translating natural growth behaviors into digital systems, the work speculates on a new design order where AI becomes the extension of nature, and humans become observers of its evolution.

Research

Post-human and Non-Human Agency

Key points:

  1. A. Humans are no longer the center of the world but part of a larger ecological and technological network.
  2. B. Matter possesses vitality and agency, capable of responding to external stimuli.
  3. C. Design should acknowledge and respond to the agency of materials and nonhuman elements, fostering a more balanced symbiotic relationship.

The post-human society refers to a new era of human-machine integration where technology deeply permeates life and reshapes human form, capabilities, and social structures.

Ecological Intelligence

Distributed Nature Intelligence

Intelligence in nature is not centralized in a single “brain,” but emerges through distributed systems such as fungal mycelia, coral colonies, forest root networks, and neural webs, all operating through three common principles.

  • Self-Organization in Microbial Systems
  • Feedback Loop in Microbial Systems
  • Nonlinear Growth in Coral System

Material experiment

Cultivation of Mycelial Networks

Ganoderma lucidum mycelium

To explore how nature is involved in morphogenesis, I took Ganoderma lucidum mycelium to observe its growth process without manual intervention.

Oat granules

Mycelium was placed on a culture medium with oat particles. Oats are “energy nodes” or “food targets”; mycelium sought, connected and occupied resource points by its logic.

Observation record

The mycelium begins exploratory extension within the first 24 hours. They actively search for food (oats) and the boundary of space. The growth path shows the coexistence of “direction + randomness”:

  1. It will avoid obstacles and choose the direction of less resistance.
  2. It will accelerate the diffusion when it encounters an energy source.
  3. Different colonies establish connections or compete with each other.

The overall morphology is from point to net to sheet to mass.

Concept

The Symbiotic World of Plants, Technology, Humans

In this future world, plants, technologies and humans form a continuous living network. Power shifts from human control to distributed ecological intelligence. Plants signal, machines interpret growth as emotion, and humans follow non-human rhythms. Forms arise through co-evolution as boundaries between organism, interface and environment dissolve into a shared, symbiotic field.

Sketch

Plant-based wearable device clothing

Inspired by natural systems that organize themselves, like the way fungi grow in networks or coral reefs form repeating patterns. These organisms show how complex structures can come from simple local actions, without any central control.

Timeline

  1. 2025-The First Connections

    Wearables merge with live plants for biofeedback and calm. Growth and emotion share the same rhythm.

  2. 2058-Vegetal Autonomy

    Plants sense and respond autonomously; designers follow vegetal networks instead of control.

  3. 2087-Hybrid Consciousness

    Biological and digital systems merge; AI translates plant signals into emotional interfaces. The boundary between algorithm and organism begins to dissolve.

  4. 2105-Self-Growing Structures

    Plant-based structures build and repair themselves through distributed feedback. Human intervention becomes minimal: nature designs its own intelligence.

  5. 2165-The Sentient Environment

    The environment itself becomes sentient; data, roots, and memories intertwine. Human presence is archived as a trace inside living ecosystems.
