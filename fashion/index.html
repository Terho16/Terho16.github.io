Reconstruction of Oracle Bone

Ancient inscriptions reconstructed as a contemporary four-look collection.

Oracle-bone inscriptions become a wearable language through modular structures, lettering, and Klein blue. The collection moves between record, ritual, and the body.

Role
Fashion design, silhouette development, styling
Collection
Four looks
Award
Excellence Award, Hempel Award 2024

Research

The visual grammar of a script

The project begins with the visual grammar of oracle-bone writing: its sharp marks, ritual presence, and connection to historical record.

Translation

From characters to structure

Tube structures and adjustable components reinterpret characters as volume, movement, and modular function.

Line-up

Four looks

All four looks side by side: black garments with Klein blue tube structures.

Recognition

Excellence Award, Hempel Award

32nd China International Young Fashion Designers Contest, 2024

022023

Ornament as Memory Device

Accessories as intimate carriers of texture, gesture, and personal meaning.

This material study treats jewelry and body-scale objects as small narrative devices. The work combines metal, beads, found forms, and graphic marks.

Role
Accessory design, material study, styling
Materials
Jade, metal, and beads
Worn
Close to the face and body

Starting point

Material, surface, gesture

Close observation of material, surface, and gesture informed a set of accessories that sits near the face and body.

Making

Hand-built, deliberately imperfect

Each object brings together hand-built details and a deliberately imperfect visual language.

Connection

This work built the tactile and narrative sensitivity that later shaped my wearable projects.
